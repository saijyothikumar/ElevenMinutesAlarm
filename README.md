<h1 align="center">Eleven Minutes Alarm</h1>

<p align="center">
  <strong>The fastest way to set a 11-minute alarm. No UI.</strong>
</p>

---

<h2 align="center">📝 About the Project</h2>

<p align="center">
I am used to setting 11-minute alarms multiple times a day. Whether it's for taking a <b>power break</b>, remembering to <b>turn off a motor pump</b>, or handling <b>small domestic tasks</b> that often slip my mind.
<br><br>
So when <b>Google</b> sent me a mail today that <b>Google Assistant</b> is going away, I knew it was time to build my own solution. I wanted something faster than voice commands and simpler than opening a clock app.
<br><br>
So, I built this no UI, single task app with the help of <b>Google's Gemini</b>. 😄
</p>

---

<h2 align="center">🚀 Features</h2>

<p align="center">
⚡ <b>Instant Setting</b> — Tap the icon, and the alarm is set. No screens to wait for.<br>
👻 <b>Zero UI</b> — A "Ghost" activity that finishes instantly after the task is done.<br>
🍞 <b>Fluent Feedback</b> — A quick Toast message confirms your alarm is ready.<br>
🛡️ <b>Accidental-Touch Protection</b> — Built-in 8-second debounce to prevent duplicate alarms.<br>
🪶 <b>Lightweight</b> — Tiny APK size with zero background battery drain.
</p>

<h2 align="center">🛠️ Technical Bit</h2>

<p align="center">
This app uses a transparent <i>Translucent</i> theme and standard Android <code>AlarmClock</code> Intents. It asks for <code>SET_ALARM</code> permission, which is granted automatically at install time. No fancy services, no tracking, just utility.
</p>
